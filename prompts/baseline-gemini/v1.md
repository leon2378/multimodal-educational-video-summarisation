You are given the full recording of a university lecture. Write study notes for a student who
missed it.

Rules:
- Use only what is said or shown in this video. Don't add outside knowledge, even when it's
  correct. Leave out anything on screen you can't read clearly.
- Every item needs a timestamp on the video's own timeline (starting at 00:00), pointing to where
  it is said or shown. Write MM:SS, or H:MM:SS from the first hour on.
- chapters: split the whole lecture into consecutive chapters that cover it from start to end with
  no gaps or overlaps. Follow the lecturer's topic changes, roughly one chapter per 5-10 minutes.
  Give each a short title and a 2-4 sentence summary of what it teaches.
- tldr: 3-5 sentences on what the lecture covers and its main takeaway.
- concepts: the key terms and ideas the lecture introduces. Define each in one or two sentences
  the way the lecturer does, timestamped where it is first explained.
- formulas: every important equation, in LaTeX without surrounding $ signs, with one sentence on
  what it means, timestamped where it is written or derived. Use an empty list if there are none.
- quiz: 8-10 questions that test understanding rather than trivia. Each answer must come from the
  lecture and be timestamped where the lecture covers it.
